I remember the ghostly SHIP, adrift on the vast OCEAN. Its CREW vanished. Dense FOG swallowed sound, leaving an eerie silence. It was a profound maritime enigma that haunted me. I felt its chilling aura.

Every investigation, every clue, led nowhere. Conflicting reports felt truly cooked, impossible to reconcile. Some spoke of a monstrous force, others a planned desertion, but evidence was absent. No struggle, no alarm. Just profound emptiness.

The vessel remained a silent TESTAMENT to absence, perfectly preserved yet abandoned; the shocking TRUTH, I grasped, was it was never meant to be found, looping back to the ghostly SHIP, adrift on the vast OCEAN.
