I was walking through the abandoned MANSION, its decaying walls breathing an unsettling aura of forgotten lives, when I stumbled upon a tarnished KEY. The air was thick with dust. My heart pounded against my RIBS.

The old DIARY lay open on a rickety table, its pages brittle with age, detailing cryptic entries about a missing jewel and a vanished heir. Someone was cooked. They simply vanished, leaving perplexing clues scattered on the FLOORBOARDS. This true enigma gave me a chilling dread.
