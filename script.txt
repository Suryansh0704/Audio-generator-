I was confronted by the true terror of the COSMOS, an unnerving sense of humanity's utter insignificance. Imagine a rogue BLACK HOLE, unseen and silent, casually drifting towards our sun, ready to swallow everything. Our PLANET gone, a mere snack. It's an indifferent, colossal force.

It genuinely enrages me how easily a gamma-ray burst from a distant, dying GALAXY could obliterate us. All life on Earth would be cooked instantly, without warning or mercy. Our history, our struggles, our brief existence, would vanish. This cosmic apathy is infuriating.

I often ponder the inevitable heat death of the universe, where all energy dissipates and everything becomes an endless, cold VOID; this slow, agonizing fade into nothingness perfectly captures the true terror of the COSMOS.
