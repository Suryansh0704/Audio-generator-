I was standing in the abandoned MANSION, cold air clinging, pondering the inexplicable disappearance of Mr. Sterling, who vanished fifty years ago, leaving a half-eaten dinner. Dust on antique FURNITURE told silent stories through the cracked WINDOW.

Detectives scoured the estate, interviewed servants, yet no forced entry or logical explanation for his sudden absence surfaced, creating an almost supernatural aura. The only peculiar item discovered was a cryptic note near a burning CANDLE.

I felt a chill, a certainty that something sinister had occurred within these walls; he was never coming back, becoming just another GHOST story whispered on a stormy night, a story that always begins with me standing in the abandoned MANSION.
