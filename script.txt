I was standing on the desolate DOCK. The wind whipped. The 'Sea Serpent' was gone, its aura a memory. The captain, a man of iron will, vanished into the FOG, leaving no trace.

The vast OCEAN swallowed them. Investigators were baffled. Experts were cooked, scratching heads over the missing SHIP. I combed logbooks, finding a cryptic NOTE on the captain's abandoned desk.

Authorities called it an accident. I knew something sinister transpired, an enigma hidden within the depths; I realized then, with a jolt, the message was not a destination, but a warning, leading me right back to the desolate DOCK.
