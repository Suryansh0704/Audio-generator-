I was standing at the threshold of the MANSION, a chilling aura emanating from its walls, twilight casting long, dancing SHADOWS. My heart pounded as I pushed open the heavy DOORWAY, its groan echoing, a whisper in the gloom.

The air was thick with dust, a strange, sweet perfume of decay clinging to every surface. I discovered a single, tarnished SILVER KEY on a dusty antique TABLE. Its purpose unknown in the empty ROOM. This place felt cooked.

Every creak sent shivers down my spine, reinforcing the profound sense of enduring mystery that plagued this house for generations, a silent watcher; the note, neatly folded, simply read, "You were always here."
