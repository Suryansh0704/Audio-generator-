I remember the chill of that medieval CASTLE, always cloaked in a foreboding aura, especially after the KNIGHT vanished. His armor remained, silent and empty. A perplexing disappearance, indeed.

The guards, utterly cooked by the lack of clues, found nothing. No footprint, no struggle. His sword lay beside an ancient CRYPT, its stone lid ajar. Witnesses offered conflicting accounts.

I kept returning to a single, unsigned LETTER found near the CRYPT, its parchment brittle, hinting at an unseen pact; the full MOON seemed to mock our efforts, and I often wonder if the KNIGHT truly vanished, or merely waits within the medieval CASTLE, cloaked in a foreboding aura.
